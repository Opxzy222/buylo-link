Frequently Asked Questions

What is Buylo?

Buylo is a local commerce platform that helps you discover businesses, products, and services around you.

You can search for what you need, discover nearby businesses, view their information and location, connect with sellers, and place orders for pickup.

Businesses can also create a Space on Buylo to showcase their products or services and become discoverable to customers.

What is a Space?

A Space is a business's presence on Buylo.

A business can use its Space to:

  • Display business information
  • Showcase products or services
  • Share posts and stories
  • Receive messages from customers
  • Become discoverable to nearby customers
  • Receive and manage orders
  • Build a relationship with customers

Think of a Space as your business's home on Buylo.

What is a Space ID?

A Space ID is the unique identifier assigned to a business Space on Buylo.

Customers can use a Space ID to find a specific business directly.

For example:

Space ID: Y035976

If a business gives you its Space ID, you can use it to locate that business on Buylo.

How do I find a product or service?

Open Buylo and search for what you're looking for.

You can discover businesses based on your location or search within a specific city or area.

For example, you could search for:

  • Perfume
  • Phones
  • Fashion
  • Furniture
  • Hair services
  • Restaurants
  • Electronics
  • Medical services
  • Beauty products
  • Repairs

Buylo helps you discover businesses that offer what you're looking for.

How does buying on Buylo work?

The process is simple:

Search → Discover → Connect → Order → Pick Up

  1. Search for the product or service you need.
  2. Discover businesses that offer it.
  3. View the business and available information.
  4. Communicate with the business if you have questions.
  5. Place an order when you're ready.
  6. Pick up your order from the business.

Always review the order details before confirming.

How do I contact a business?

Open the business's Space and use the available communication options to contact the business.

You can ask questions about products, availability, pricing, pickup, and other details before placing an order.

How do I follow a business?

Open the business's Space and tap Follow.

Following a business allows you to stay updated with content the business shares on Buylo, including posts and stories.

Account Problems

I can't sign in to my account

If you're having trouble signing in:

  1. Make sure you're using the correct phone number or account details.
  2. Check that your internet connection is working.
  3. Make sure you're using the latest version of Buylo.
  4. Close and reopen the app.
  5. Try signing in again.

If you still can't access your account, contact Buylo Support.

I can't verify my account

Some Buylo activities may require account verification.

If verification isn't working:

  • Make sure your information is entered correctly.
  • Make sure your identification details match the information you're providing.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Try again later if the verification service is temporarily unavailable.

If the problem continues, contact support and describe the issue you're experiencing.

Never send your password, OTP, PIN, or other confidential security information to anyone claiming to be Buylo Support.

I changed my phone number

If you've changed the phone number associated with your account, contact Buylo Support if you're unable to access or update your account.

For your security, we may need to verify that you're the account owner before making certain account-related changes.

My account has been restricted

If you believe your account has been incorrectly restricted or you're unable to use certain Buylo features, contact support.

Tell us:

  • The account you're having trouble with
  • What you're trying to do
  • What happens when you try
  • Any error message you receive

Our team will review the issue.

Business Account Help

How do I create a business Space?

You can create a business Space directly through Buylo.

Provide your business information and complete the setup process.

Once your Space is created, you can add information about your business and begin building your presence on Buylo.

What can I add to my Space?

Depending on the features available to your account, you can add:

  • Business name
  • Business description
  • Location
  • Opening hours
  • Products
  • Services
  • Prices
  • Photos
  • Posts
  • Stories
  • Business information
  • Contact options

Keep your business information accurate and up to date.

How do I update my business information?

Open My Space and access your business management options.

From there, you can update the information available for your business Space.

If you're unable to make a change, contact Buylo Support.

How do I find my Space ID?

Your Space ID is displayed within your business Space.

You can share your Space ID with customers so they can find your business directly on Buylo.

How do customers find my business?

Customers can discover businesses through Buylo's search and discovery features.

A customer may search for:

  • Your products
  • Your services
  • Your business
  • A category
  • Businesses within a specific area

Make sure your Space contains accurate business information and relevant products or services to make it easier for customers to understand what you offer.

I can't create my business Space

If you're having trouble creating a Space:

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Make sure all required information has been completed.
  3. Make sure your business information follows Buylo's guidelines.
  4. Update Buylo to the latest version.
  5. Try again.

If the problem continues, contact support.

How do I report a fake or misleading business?

If you believe a business is providing misleading information, pretending to be another business, engaging in suspicious activity, or violating Buylo's rules, you can report the business.

Use the Report option available on the relevant business Space or contact Buylo Support.

Provide as much useful information as possible so our team can investigate.

Order Problems

Where can I find my orders?

Your orders and related purchase information can be accessed through the relevant order or receipt sections of your Buylo account.

My order hasn't been accepted

Businesses may need time to review and respond to an order.

If your order remains pending longer than expected, you can contact the business directly.

If you believe there is a technical problem with the order, contact Buylo Support.

My order was rejected

An order may be rejected by a business for several reasons, such as availability or other business-related circumstances.

If you need clarification, contact the business directly.

If you believe the order was rejected because of a technical problem with Buylo, contact support.

I need to cancel my order

If you need to cancel an order, check whether cancellation is available for that order.

If cancellation isn't available, contact the business as soon as possible.

Buylo may not be able to cancel an order on your behalf once the business has accepted or started processing it.

The product I ordered is unavailable

If a business tells you that an ordered product is unavailable, contact the business to discuss the available options.

Depending on the situation, the business may offer an alternative or another resolution.

I didn't receive my order

If you were expecting an order and it wasn't available for pickup:

  1. Contact the business first.
  2. Confirm the order details and pickup information.
  3. If the issue cannot be resolved, contact Buylo Support.

Include your order or receipt information when contacting support.

My pickup code isn't working

If your pickup code isn't being accepted:

  • Confirm that you're using the correct code.
  • Check that you're at the correct business.
  • Confirm that the order is ready for pickup.
  • Ask the business to verify the order.

If the issue continues, contact Buylo Support.

Do not share your pickup code with anyone other than the business handling your order.

Payment & Receipts

Does Buylo provide digital receipts?

Yes. Purchases made through Buylo can generate digital purchase receipts that help you keep a record of your transactions.

Your receipts can help you review:

  • Business name
  • Purchase amount
  • Order information
  • Purchase date
  • Order status
  • Other available transaction details

Where can I find my receipts?

Go to the Customer Receipts section of your Buylo account.

Your receipts can be organized by transaction status and date, making it easier to find previous purchases.

Can I track my spending?

Buylo's receipt system can help you keep track of purchases recorded through the platform.

Your receipts can provide a convenient overview of your purchase history and spending.

My receipt is incorrect

If information on your receipt appears incorrect, first contact the business associated with the transaction.

If the information is related to a Buylo technical issue, contact our support team with the relevant receipt or order information.

I can't find my receipt

If you can't find a receipt:

  1. Check your Customer Receipts section.
  2. Check the date range you're viewing.
  3. Make sure you're signed into the correct account.
  4. Check whether the purchase was completed successfully.

If the receipt is still missing, contact Buylo Support.

I was charged incorrectly

If you believe you were charged an incorrect amount, contact the business first to confirm the transaction details.

If you believe the issue is related to Buylo's platform or payment processing, contact support and provide the relevant transaction information.

Never send your card number, PIN, password, or OTP through support messages.

Report a Business

When should I report a business?

You should consider reporting a business if you believe it:

  • Is impersonating another business
  • Provides misleading business information
  • Uses inappropriate or prohibited content
  • Engages in suspicious activity
  • Attempts to scam customers
  • Posts fraudulent or misleading information
  • Violates Buylo's rules
  • Creates an unsafe experience for customers or other businesses

How do I report a business?

Open the business's Space and use the available reporting option.

Provide a clear explanation of the problem and any relevant information that can help us understand what happened.

What happens after I report a business?

Reports are reviewed by Buylo where appropriate.

Depending on the result of the review, Buylo may take action in accordance with its policies and applicable rules.

Submitting a report does not automatically mean that a business will be removed.

Can I report a business anonymously?

Where available, Buylo may allow reports to be submitted without publicly identifying the person who made the report.

However, information submitted to Buylo may be reviewed internally to investigate the report.

Before You Contact Support

You may be able to solve the problem faster by checking:

  1. Update Buylo — Make sure you're using the latest version of the app.
  2. Check your internet connection — Some Buylo features require an active internet connection.
  3. Restart the app — Close Buylo completely and open it again.
  4. Check your account — Make sure you're signed into the correct Buylo account.
  5. Check the business directly — For product availability, pickup arrangements, pricing, and other business-specific questions, contacting the business directly may be the fastest option.

Buylo Safety

Your safety matters to us.

When using Buylo:

  • Review business information before making a purchase.
  • Confirm product and order details before accepting an order.
  • Keep your account credentials private.
  • Never share your OTP, PIN, password, or pickup code unnecessarily.
  • Be careful with requests for payments outside the agreed purchasing process.
  • Report suspicious businesses or activity to Buylo.
  • Contact support if something doesn't feel right.

Buylo provides a platform for customers and businesses to connect. Always use your best judgment when interacting with another party.
