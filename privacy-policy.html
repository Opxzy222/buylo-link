Buylo Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

At Buylo, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how GOGO DIGITAL MARKET LTD ("Buylo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use the Buylo mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By using Buylo, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. About Buylo

Buylo is a local commerce platform that helps users discover businesses, products, and services around them.

Through Buylo, users may:

  • Search for products and services
  • Discover nearby businesses
  • View business information and locations
  • Contact businesses
  • Follow businesses
  • View posts and stories
  • Place orders for pickup
  • Receive digital purchase receipts
  • Track purchase history
  • Manage their account

Businesses may use Buylo to:

  • Create a business Space
  • Add business information
  • List products and services
  • Publish posts and stories
  • Communicate with customers
  • Receive and manage orders
  • Become discoverable to customers

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Buylo, we may collect different categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

When you create or manage a Buylo account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Username or account identifier
  • Profile information
  • Account credentials or authentication information
  • Account verification status

We use this information to create and manage your account, provide our Services, communicate with you, and maintain account security.

3. Identity Verification

Some Buylo features may require identity verification.

To help verify the identity of users and improve trust and security on the platform, Buylo uses QoreID, an identity-verification service provided by a third party.

Depending on the verification method used, the verification process may involve information such as:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Government-issued identification information
  • National Identification Number (NIN), where applicable
  • Verification information or verification results
  • Other information required by the selected verification method

QoreID's documentation indicates that its identity-verification services may use government identity information and return verification results based on the verification process performed.

Why we use identity verification

We may use identity verification to:

  • Confirm that an account belongs to a real person
  • Protect users and businesses from fraud
  • Reduce fake or fraudulent accounts
  • Protect the integrity of the Buylo marketplace
  • Meet security and legal requirements
  • Support certain account or transaction-related features

We do not use identity-verification information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or informing you where required.

QoreID processes information according to its own privacy practices and applicable data-protection obligations. You can review QoreID's privacy policy for more information about how QoreID processes personal data.

4. Location Information

Buylo is designed around local discovery.

With your permission, we may collect or access location information from your device.

Location information may be used to:

  • Show businesses near you
  • Help you search for nearby products and services
  • Calculate or display distances
  • Improve local search results
  • Show businesses within a selected area
  • Provide directions or location-related functionality

You can control location permissions through your device settings.

If you disable location access, some Buylo features that depend on your location may not work as intended.

5. Business Information

If you create or manage a business Space, we may collect information such as:

  • Business name
  • Business description
  • Business category
  • Business address
  • Business location
  • Business phone number
  • Business email address
  • Opening hours
  • Products
  • Services
  • Prices
  • Business photos
  • Business posts
  • Business stories
  • Space ID
  • Other information you choose to publish

Some business information may be publicly visible to Buylo users because the purpose of a business Space is to help customers discover and interact with the business.

Business owners are responsible for ensuring that information they publish on Buylo is accurate and that they have the necessary rights to publish it.

6. Content You Provide

Buylo allows users and businesses to submit content.

This may include:

  • Messages
  • Product information
  • Business information
  • Photos
  • Videos
  • Posts
  • Stories
  • Reviews or ratings
  • Questions
  • Support requests
  • Other information submitted through the platform

We process this information to provide the relevant Buylo features and services.

For example, messages sent to a business may be made available to that business so that it can respond to you.

7. Orders and Purchase Information

When you place an order through Buylo, we may collect information associated with that order, including:

  • Buyer information
  • Business information
  • Products or services ordered
  • Quantity
  • Price
  • Order status
  • Order date
  • Pickup information
  • Pickup code or related order identifier
  • Transaction or receipt information

We use this information to:

  • Process and manage orders
  • Notify users and businesses about order updates
  • Support pickup
  • Generate digital receipts
  • Maintain purchase records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

8. Payments

Buylo may provide features that allow users and businesses to manage purchase-related information.

Where a payment service provider is used, payment information may be processed by the relevant third-party payment provider.

Buylo does not intend to store your full payment-card number, card PIN, or similar highly sensitive payment credentials on its own systems unless expressly stated otherwise.

If Buylo integrates a payment provider in the future, the applicable provider's privacy policy and terms may also apply to information processed by that provider.

9. Digital Receipts

Buylo may generate digital receipts for purchases made through the platform.

Receipts may contain information such as:

  • Business name
  • Products or services purchased
  • Purchase amount
  • Date of purchase
  • Order information
  • Order status
  • Other transaction details

We use receipt information to help you keep track of your purchases and to provide transaction records.

10. Messages and Communications

Buylo may provide communication features that allow customers and businesses to communicate directly.

When you send a message through Buylo, the message and associated information may be processed and made available to the intended recipient.

We may process communications to:

  • Deliver messages
  • Maintain the functionality of our messaging system
  • Protect users
  • Investigate abuse or violations
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect and prevent fraud or harmful activity

Users should avoid sharing passwords, PINs, OTPs, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary Buylo messages.

11. Device and Technical Information

When you use Buylo, we may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Device identifiers where applicable
  • Network information
  • Crash information
  • Diagnostic information
  • General usage information
  • Log information

We use this information to:

  • Keep Buylo functioning
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve performance
  • Protect account and platform security
  • Detect malicious activity
  • Understand how our Services are being used

We may use personal information to:

Communicate with you

  • Account notifications
  • Order updates

Comply with legal obligations

13. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, Buylo may process personal data on one or more lawful bases recognized under applicable data-protection law, including:

  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to provide a service you requested.
  • Consent — where you have given consent for a specific processing activity.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required by applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests — where processing is necessary for legitimate business or security purposes and those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Protection of vital interests — where applicable.
  • Other lawful bases permitted by applicable data-protection law.

Nigeria's data-protection framework recognizes the importance of lawful, fair, transparent, purpose-limited, necessary, accurate, and secure processing of personal data.

We may share information where necessary to provide the Services, protect users, operate our platform, or comply with applicable law.

16. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Unauthorized disclosure
  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Alteration
  • Destruction

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and authentication information confidential.

17. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including to:

  • Provide our Services
  • Maintain business and transaction records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud
  • Maintain security
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Enforce agreements

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Where personal information is no longer required and there is no legal or legitimate reason to retain it, we will take reasonable steps to delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it.

18. Identity Verification Data Retention

Identity-verification information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the verification, security, fraud-prevention, legal, regulatory, and record-keeping purposes for which it was collected.

The information processed by QoreID may also be subject to QoreID's applicable retention policies and legal obligations.

We do not retain identity information longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which we are legally permitted to retain it.

The exact retention period for identity-verification information may depend on the verification method and applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

If your app provides an in-app account deletion option, you may use that option directly from your account settings.

Apple requires apps that support account creation to provide users with a way to initiate account deletion within the app.
