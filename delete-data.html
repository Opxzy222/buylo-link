Delete Your Account & Data

You can permanently delete your Buylo account and associated data directly from the app.

1 How to delete your account

  1. Open the Buylo app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap on Delete Account
  4. Enter your account password to confirm
Important: After you confirm deletion, your account will be scheduled for permanent deletion and will be automatically deleted after 7 days.

2 What happens after deletion

  1. Your account will be deactivated immediately
  2. You will no longer be able to log in
  3. After 7 days, your personal data and account will be permanently deleted

3 Need help?

If you are unable to delete your account from the app or need assistance, you can contact us at:

support@buylo.com.ng
